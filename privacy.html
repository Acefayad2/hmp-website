Privacy.

HMP Luxury Event Services collects the information you provide through the inquiry form so we can review your celebration details, prepare a quote, and respond to you.

Information we collect

Your submission may include your name, email address, phone number, event dates, event location, service interests, guest count, and other planning details you choose to share.

How we use it

We use this information to communicate with you about your inquiry and requested services. We do not sell the information submitted through this website.

Storage and service providers

Inquiry submissions are processed through Netlify Forms and may be retained in HMP’s business email and service records as needed to respond and provide requested services.

Your choices

You may ask HMP to correct or delete information you submitted by emailing info@hmpeds.com.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact HMP Luxury Event Services at info@hmpeds.com or 301-471-0990.
