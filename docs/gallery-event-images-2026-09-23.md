## Prompt set

Shared direction: photorealistic-natural editorial event photography for a luxury event-services website. People-free scenes, realistic textures, restrained warm lighting. No logos, watermarks, collage, borders, or readable text.

- Corporate: leadership reception in a modern ballroom, presentation stage and blank screen, ivory-linen cocktail tables, walnut details, understated white flowers, candlelight, warm cream and plum palette. Vertical 4:5 composition with room depth, avoiding artificial CGI gloss.
- Engagement: intimate party on a garden terrace at dusk, champagne coupe glasses, small ivory cake, blush and ivory flowers, candles, softly lit backdrop. Party reception rather than a wedding ceremony. Landscape 3:2 composition with the styled table centered for cropping, warm cream, champagne, muted blush and subtle plum accents.
- Graduation: sophisticated event venue with a prominent black mortarboard and gold tassel, rolled diploma tied with ribbon, ivory cake, black/champagne-gold/ivory balloon arrangement and flowers. Landscape 3:2 composition, natural daylight with warm accents. No school logos or year numerals.
