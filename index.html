About HMP

Collecting money for an event shouldn't feel like guesswork

Between cash handed off in person, Zelle screenshots buried in a group chat, and spreadsheets that go stale the moment someone forgets to update them, it's nearly impossible to know exactly who's paid and how much you've actually collected.

For organizers

One dashboard for every event — no more chasing payment screenshots across five apps.

For organizations

Churches, nonprofits, and teams get a shared source of truth their whole team can trust.

For accountability

Every payment has proof attached and a receipt trail — nothing gets lost or disputed.

How It Works
